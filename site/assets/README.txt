Deepavali greeting card and page assets

favicon-pv.svg, prajavani-white.svg   Prajavani favicon and logo (header and card band)

deepavali/   Greeting card artwork, generated by tools/build-art.py from _artwork-masters/. Do not edit
             these by hand: replace the master and rerun the script.

  {kurta|panche|saree|langa}-{diya|sparkler}.webp   Outfits, 1080 x 1110 (scene coordinates: drawn at
                                                     y 150 on the 1080 x 1350 card). Arms, hands and the
                                                     diya or sparkler are part of the picture.
  {outfit}-{pose}-skin.png                           Where each outfit's skin is (white). The card
                                                     recolours those pixels to the visitor's skin tone.
  bg-{doorstep|night-sky|fireworks|lakshmi-puja|balipadyami}.webp
                                                     Card backgrounds, 1080 x 1350.
  bg-{scene}-thumb.webp                              Thumbnails for the background picker.

The neck opening of each outfit is measured by tools/build-art.py; after new outfit artwork, copy the
NECKS line it prints into site/greeting.js.
